# Pricing arguments

Use these points when a customer asks why a quote costs what it does. They are
used by the [sales area](../areas/sales.md) and were collected for the
[pilot](../projects/northern-build-pilot.md).

1. Every quote is a fixed price. If the work costs more, Northern Build pays
   the difference.
2. Every job has a named site manager. The name is written on the quote.
3. Workmanship carries a five-year warranty.
4. Discounts: a salesperson may give up to 5 % alone. Above that, the owner
   signs.

## Decisions

- 2026-10-09: The four points above are the standard price arguments. Source:
  [owner's e-mail](../../sources/2026-10-09-pricing-email.md).
